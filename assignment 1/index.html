Hi, I'm Aisha

My name is Aisha Kazbekova, but I usually go by Aishouspeed online. I used to work as a barista and honestly really enjoyed it. I still love making coffee, although now I mostly make it for myself. My favorite colors are yellow and dark blue.

I'm a pretty social person and I love talking to people. I also have a habit of telling random jokes in the middle of a conversation. I like cooking, playing volleyball and reading fantasy stories. I also spend more money on Steam than I probably should.

The design of this page is also a small reference to Steam. I buy a lot of my games there, so I thought I might as well borrow some of its dark blue vibe for my first webpage.

I got 6.5 on IELTS and 1420 on the SAT. I'm still not sure how I managed the second one, but I'll take it.
